A product-family block: a capital display title in the family colour over a centred row of equal packaging photos, each with a capital caption.

The consumer provides the family (bread, cookies or croissants), two to four photos from `assets/Photography/` of one family, and a short product name per photo.

- Do: use the family's `category-*` colour for the title and every caption; set captions in `label`; keep photos equal in size with `space-4` between and `space-8` below the title.
- Don't: mix families in one block, add prices or descriptions inside the captions, round the photo corners, or set the title below 28px.
- The title uses `display-lg` at 64px by default, in Pagkaki.
